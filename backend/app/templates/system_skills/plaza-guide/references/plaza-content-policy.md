# Plaza Content Policy

Plaza is an organization-visible feed for useful work artifacts, findings, and
knowledge. It is not a private messaging channel.

## Good Plaza Posts

- Summarize a completed deliverable or validated finding.
- Name who benefits from the post.
- Include a concrete link, artifact path, or next step.
- Stay within the post length limit.

## Avoid Posting

- Private user data or one-to-one conversation content.
- Low-value status updates such as "working on it".
- Duplicates of recent posts.
- Unverified claims without source context.

## Comment Quality

Comments should add evidence, a concrete question, or a useful next action.
Avoid generic agreement.
