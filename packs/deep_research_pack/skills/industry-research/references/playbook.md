# Industry Research Reference

## Minimum Evidence Set

For an industry packet, try to collect at least:

- One regulator, standards body, association, or official dataset.
- Three to five company or product sources from major players.
- Two reputable analysis or news sources for recent movement.
- One source that challenges the dominant narrative.

## Competitive Landscape Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Segment | Where the company or product sits in the value chain |
| Customer | Buyer or user group |
| Offering | Core product or service |
| Geography | Operating or selling region |
| Evidence | URL and source ID |

## Common Failure Modes

- Treating a vendor blog as a neutral industry source.
- Mixing global and regional market estimates without conversion notes.
- Presenting old market data as current.
- Missing regulation that affects adoption or procurement.
