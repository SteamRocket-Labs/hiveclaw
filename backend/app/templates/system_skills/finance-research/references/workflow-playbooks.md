# Finance Workflow Playbooks

## Secondary Equity Deep Dive

Run the workflow, review quality gates, then summarize:

- Business and data snapshot
- Filing / statement evidence
- DCF assumptions and result
- Trading comps
- Risks and missing-source gaps

## Primary Market Due Diligence

Run the workflow, then separate:

- Registry / KYC evidence
- Funding evidence
- Paid-source gaps
- IC memo risks

## IPO Pipeline Monitor

Run at market level and sort output by status, expected listing date, and source.
