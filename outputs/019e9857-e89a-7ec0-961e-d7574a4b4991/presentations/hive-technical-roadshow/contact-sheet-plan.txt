Claim spine:
1. Hive is not a chatbot wrapper; it is self-evolving digital employee infrastructure plus an enterprise control plane.
2. The technical moat is the combination of persistent agent identity, governed memory, durable runtime, deterministic workflow, and auditable tool execution.
3. The model remains fully capable; the harness constrains authority and side effects, not intelligence.
4. The current repo already has a substantial platform surface: multi-tenant FastAPI/React app, 100+ tool registrations, Memory Control Plane, Office, channels, skills/MCP, subagents, Plan Mode, Workflow, and Deep Research workstreams.
5. The next roadshow story is technical credibility: show the runtime, trust boundaries, and evidence rather than vague AI productivity claims.
